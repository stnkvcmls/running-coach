Running Coach: less grey, same rules

The app reads grey because the approved rule "colour means effort only" is doing its job. Most screens have no effort on them, so they render in ink on cement. This proposal keeps that rule and fixes the grey with three things. Effort colour goes everywhere effort already exists. A lighter ground with hairlines replaces the cement. One new hue, petrol, carries exactly one meaning: your body.

Why it reads grey

This is the joint diagnosis. Gemini found causes 1–4 on its own. Claude added cause 5 in round 2.

  1. Cement ground. With no shadows and no borders, --bg #ECEEF0 had to be dark enough to separate panels by tone alone. It then floods the top bar, the Coach canvas, and every gap between cards. In dark mode, #1A1E21 on #111416 merges into one slab outdoors.
  2. No-effort screens have no colour at all. Below the session hero, Today (readiness, body, form, races, coach) is fully achromatic. So are Progress (five black sparklines on grey bands) and Coach.
  3. Effort colour is missing even where effort exists. Plan's week mini-bars and week-km bar are ink. Its phase timeline is a row of identical grey segments, and workouts get only an 8px dot. The run's HR trace is black over a grey area fill.
  4. Every emphasis is ink. Buttons, the readiness meter, fitness lines and range markers all share one ink colour. In dark mode the primary button is a flat #E8EBED bar.
  5. Grey means two things. Z1 effort is grey, and so is "inactive" (missed, rest, planned, empty tracks). That makes grey the most common mark in the app, and it can't mean Z1 reliably.

Today

  1. Lighter ground, hairline panels. #F3F4F6 ground, white cards with a 1px #DCE0E4 line. The top bar moves onto the card colour, so the cement band at the top disappears.
  2. Readiness is petrol. It's about your body, not a session, so it gets the body hue instead of ink.
  3. The hero gets its session's colour. The divider between readiness and the session becomes a 3px zone rule, and the badge is tinted. The label stays in ink. The rule isn't on the card's top edge, so red never sits above the petrol readiness meter. No full-card wash, which glares outdoors.
  4. Week rail: grey only means Z1. Missed Monday is dashed in its own zone colour, not grey. Rest is a hollow outline. Planned fills go from 16% to 22%.
  5. Body flags invert. An out-of-range value gets an ink pill. An in-range value gets a petrol dot. Neither uses green or red, which would read as Z3/Z5.
  6. Form: petrol fitness line, no grey smoke. Fatigue stays as an ink dash.

Plan

  1. The current phase is filled. Build is ink and labelled in bold. Previously every segment was the same grey.
  2. Week mini-bars are drawn in effort colour. Each week's shape now reads as its intensity mix, the same strip as Today. Done is solid, planned is 50% opacity, and missed is a zone outline.
  3. The week-km bar is stacked by session. Done is solid zone, planned is a 22% tint with a zone outline, and missed is dashed. You can see where the 73 km comes from.
  4. A 4px zone bar down each row's left edge replaces the 8px dot. The bar encodes session type, and the right column already says "✓ Done", so planned rows keep a solid bar. Missed is a dashed bar, and its text stays readable at ink-2 (8.2:1) instead of the faint grey it uses now (3.2:1).

Run analysis

  1. The HR trace is stroked by zone. The line changes colour at your zone thresholds (Z1 to Z5, here Z2 < 150, Z3 < 160, Z4 < 167, Z5 ≥ 167), so the chart shows where each rep tipped into Z5. The line is a 5-point rolling mean so the colour doesn't flicker on noise.
  2. No grey area fill. Rep bands go neutral. They're a light --border tint, so a pink band doesn't fight a green stroke.
  3. Reps, time-in-zone and the red header rule already used effort colour. They don't change.

Progress · Body

  1. Body trends are petrol. The line and the end marker are petrol. The usual-range band is a petrol tint instead of grey.
  2. Out of range = ink pill + filled ink end-dot, the same signal as on Today.
  3. The selected tab stays ink. Petrol never marks a control.

The rule, amended: three channels, one meaning each

This replaces §5.1 principle 1 and the "Shadows go" note in §5.2. Everything else in §5 stands.

Zones = effort of a session

Unchanged hexes, Garmin order. Now also used on structural marks: the hero session rule, 14% badge tints, row bars, week mini-bars, the stacked week-km bar, and the HR stroke. Still never used for text. A solid grey mark means Z1 and nothing else.

Petrol = your body

Readiness meter, the Form fitness line, HRV, resting-HR and sleep range bands, Progress body trends, and the "in your usual range" marker. Marks only, never an action. At hue 185°, it's the only free hue that is neither violet (the stock AI-app colour) nor next to a zone. It sits ΔE ≈ 45 from Z2 and Z3. That's why it's kept off buttons, which sit next to blue Easy rows.

Ink = text, actions, selection

Primary buttons, the coach send button, active nav, selected chips and weeks, the active phase, and out-of-range pills. The dark-mode button brightens to #F3F5F7, so it reads as a button instead of a grey bar.

Inactive states are coded by shape, not by grey

  • Done: solid zone.
  • Planned: 22% zone fill + 1.5px zone border on the week rail and the week-km bar; 50% opacity on mini-bars. Plan rows keep a solid bar (type, not state).
  • Missed: hollow, dashed in its own zone colour (a zone outline on 4px mini-bars). The label is ink-2 (readable), not ink-3.
  • Rest: hollow 1px --border outline; no zone bar in Plan rows.
  • Empty tracks: --border.

What we explicitly won't do

  • No green/red for good/bad wellness. It collides with Z3/Z5.
  • No effort-coloured text. Every zone except Z5 is under 4.5:1 on white (3.0–4.4:1).
  • No full-card colour washes. They glare outdoors and turn every Easy day blue.
  • No cream or sand ground and no serif. That's the current "tasteful AI default", and it yellows in sunlight.
  • No pure #000/#FFF pairing and no novelty numerals. That was the rejected Nothing OS skin.
  • No petrol on buttons or nav. It would sit beside Z2 blue and stop meaning anything.

How we got here

Gemini, round 1 (independent; it read §5, globals.css and the 7 live screenshots)

Diagnosed causes 1–4. Proposed P1 chalk/obsidian ground + 1px borders, P2 a "Deep Petrol" accent for buttons, active nav, readiness and fitness, P3 effort colour on structure, P4 zone-stroked HR with no grey smoke, and P5 ink pills for wellness flags. Named its own biggest risk: petrol next to Z2 blue outdoors.

Claude, round 2

Re-computed Gemini's contrast numbers; all held. Measured petrol at ΔE76 47/45 from Z2 and 42/46 from Z3, against 85 between Z2 and Z3, so petrol sits halfway between two zones. Accepted P1, P3, P4. Countered P1: the ground at #F7F7F8 is only 1.07:1 against white, so use #F3F4F6 with a #DCE0E4 border. Narrowed P2: petrol gets one meaning, your body, and never marks an action. Buttons stay ink, and the dark-mode button gets the brighter ink. Added P6 (grey means only Z1; inactive states coded by shape) and P7 (3px zone rule on the hero, no wash). Cut P5 down to its colour items.

Gemini, round 2

Accepted P1–P7 with no counters, and wrote out the consolidated token and state spec this page implements. It agreed that limiting petrol to physiology removes its own Easy-day risk and keeps one hue to one meaning.

Gemini, review of this page

Gemini read this HTML and light/dark screenshots of every pair. Verdict: sign off with changes. The tokens matched 100%, and it judged the after side to solve "too greyish" while improving readability. It flagged:

  • missed and planned segments in the week-km bar didn't follow the state spec;
  • the hero's red top rule sat above the petrol readiness meter;
  • mini-bars drew planned weeks as solid;
  • the HR stroke flickered on noise, with green fighting pink rep bands;
  • five text errors: a contrast figure, a segment count, the "only free hue" claim, Z2's text contrast, and calling a missed day a grey track.
Claude, final

Accepted and applied all of the above. The session rule moved to the divider between readiness and the session. The HR line uses a 5-point mean with full Z1–Z5 stops and neutral rep bands. Planned and missed segments follow the state spec. All five text errors are fixed. Declined one point, hollow bars for planned Plan rows: the bar encodes session type, the row already says "✓ Done", and a list of hollow 4px bars would make Plan grey again. Gemini's remaining note is that Coach and the bottom nav aren't mocked. Under this rule they stay ink (send button, active tab), so there's nothing new to show.
